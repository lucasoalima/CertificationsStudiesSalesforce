Question 1
A company needs to unify customer profiles coming from several systems. Which Data 360 feature should a consultant use?
A. Data Streams
B. Identity Resolution
C. Segmentation
D. Activation
Answer: B
Explanation: Identity Resolution matches and reconciles source profiles into unified profiles.
